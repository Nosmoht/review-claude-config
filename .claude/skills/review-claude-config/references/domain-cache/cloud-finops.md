# Cloud FinOps — Domain Best Practices

- **Scopes (2025):** Framework extends beyond public cloud to SaaS (90% of orgs), licensing (64%), private cloud (57%), data centers (48%), and AI (98%).
- **22 Capabilities across 4 Domains:** Visibility & Allocation, Planning & Forecasting, Optimization & Efficiency, Governance & Automation.
- **Governance is #1 priority:** Policy and governance enforcement ranked top FinOps priority; 50% cite workload optimization/waste reduction.
- **Three-phase lifecycle:** Inform (visibility, allocation, benchmarking) -> Optimize (waste reduction, right-sizing, commitment) -> Operate (KPIs, governance policies, automation).
- **Signal-driven approach aligns:** Structured signals mapping to action classes mirror the Crawl/Walk/Run maturity model.
- **AI spend governance emerging:** AI management nearly universal concern (98%); treat as governed spend bucket.
- **Data accuracy mandate:** Source-backed claims and anomaly detection are core capabilities.
