# Commercial Pricing — Domain Best Practices

- **Benchmark vs. Decision Separation**: Market benchmarks (hourly rates, competitor pricing) are context inputs, not decision drivers. Pricing decisions must incorporate target margin, utilization rate (typically 50-60% billable), and portfolio strategy.
- **Qualification Gates**: Effective decision gates require: (1) explicit entry criteria, (2) a named decision to be made, (3) required inputs documented, (4) clear responsibility assignment. McKinsey recommends 5-10 gates per project lifecycle.
- **Audience-Specific Outputs**: Sales-facing outputs emphasize value and outcomes; recruiting-facing outputs show cost structure and margin sustainability. Never conflate audiences in a single report.
- **Pricing Tier Architecture**: Structure pricing in tiers (entry/mid/premium) mapped to distinct service scope and margin targets.
- **Profitability Tracking**: Track gross margin percentage per client/engagement. GPM guides pricing decisions for contract vs. permanent roles.
