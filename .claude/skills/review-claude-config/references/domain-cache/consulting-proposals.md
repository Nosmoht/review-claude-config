# Consulting Proposals — Domain Best Practices

- **Essential sections:** Executive Summary, Business Goals/Problem Statement, Scope & Deliverables, Timeline with milestones, Staffing/Team, Investment/Pricing, Assumptions & Exclusions, Next Steps/CTA, Legal Terms.
- **Tone and clarity:** Client-facing language, avoid internal jargon, tailor executive summary to client's pain points. Answer "why us" and "why now."
- **Traceability and governance:** Leading firms tie proposals to prior discovery/qualification data. Gate-based generation from qualified opportunity records is an advanced pattern.
- **Scope creep protection:** Explicitly state in-scope and out-of-scope items.
- **Automated generation (2025-2026):** AI-assisted tools extract structured data from meetings/CRM and generate from templates. Key enablers: schema-driven data, templatized output, version tracking.
- **DevOps-specific (2026):** Address AI-assisted CI/CD, platform engineering, DevSecOps, IaC, sovereign cloud. Emphasize partnership and knowledge transfer over tool setup.
