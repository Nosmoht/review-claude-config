# CV Recruitment — Domain Best Practices

- Structured profiles require explicit data field mapping: skills with proficiency levels, certifications, experience classifications, and role context. Poor mapping causes deduplication failures and unreliable matching scores.
- Skills taxonomy should be hierarchical (essential/desirable/optional) with proficiency ratings; skills matrices enable rapid consultant-to-project matching.
- Decision gates must separate automation from human judgment: automate data enrichment and initial filtering, gate communications and offer decisions with human approval.
- Profile quality signals include: field completeness tracking, evidence strength per claim, provenance of extracted data, and recency indicators.
- Internal vs. external profiles require differentiated handling: internal consultants need utilization tracking (target 85%), career alignment, and workload sustainability metrics; external candidates need ATS-compatible parsing, bias mitigation, and consent management.
- Staffing workflows should follow sequential gates: requirements definition, expertise matching, capacity verification, cost-benefit analysis.
- Audit trails and reason codes for all automated decisions enable recruiter override and compliance (GDPR/CCPA).
- Specialization claims need multi-signal validation (2+ supporting signals or 1 strong explicit role signal) to prevent keyword stuffing.
