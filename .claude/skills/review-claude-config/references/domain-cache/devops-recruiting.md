# DevOps Recruiting — Domain Best Practices

- German DevOps market: median time-to-hire 32 days; Berlin (39%), Munich (23%), Hamburg (15%) lead openings. Senior salaries EUR 80K+ permanent, freelance EUR 102-105/h.
- GDPR compliance requires: lawful basis documentation, data minimization, explicit consent or legitimate interest basis, candidate notification within 30 days, 6-12 month retention limits, opt-out mechanisms, audit trails.
- Cumulative GDPR fines exceeded EUR 5.88 billion by mid-2025; recruitment-specific enforcement increasing.
- Scraping emails from job portals without consent is explicitly non-compliant. Public profile visibility does not grant contact permission.
- AI-assisted candidate scoring must be transparent and explainable per Art. 13-14; candidates have right to contest automated decisions (Art. 22).
- Key sourcing platforms: freelancermap (dominant freelance), LinkedIn (international), XING (DACH, declining), StepStone (permanent).
