# NIS2/KRITIS Compliance — Domain Best Practices

- BSIG 2025 (NIS2-Umsetzungsgesetz) in force since 06.12.2025; ~29,000 entities now in scope (up from ~4,500). BSI registration deadline 3 months after entry into force.
- §30 BSIG mandates 10 minimum security measures: risk analysis, incident handling, BCM, supply chain security, secure development, vulnerability management, effectiveness assessment, cyber hygiene/training, cryptography, and access control (incl. MFA).
- Incident reporting: 24h early warning, 72h detailed notification, 30-day final report to BSI (§31 BSIG).
- Fines: up to EUR 10M or 2% global turnover (essential entities), EUR 7M (important entities). Personal management liability under §38 BSIG.
- ISO 27001/BSI-Grundschutz covers ~70-80% of NIS2 requirements; gaps mainly in incident reporting timelines, supply chain security, and BSI registration.
- Automation reduces implementation from 6-12 months to 2-4 months. Key targets: continuous risk assessment, evidence collection, incident triage for 24h window, supply chain monitoring.
- Cross-validation against primary legal text (gesetze-im-internet.de) is essential; secondary sources may lag behind amendments.
