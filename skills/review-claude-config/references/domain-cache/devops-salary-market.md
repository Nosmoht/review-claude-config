# DevOps Salary Market — Domain Best Practices

- **Multi-source triangulation is mandatory.** Blend formal surveys, job portal aggregates, and recruiter data, then cross-validate. Single-source introduces self-reported bias.
- **Normalize to common unit.** All values EUR brutto/Jahr with explicit FTE adjustment.
- **Percentile positioning over averages.** P25/P50/P75 are industry standard; averages skewed by outliers.
- **Germany-specific: KMU vs. Konzern split is critical.** Pay gaps of 20-30% between SMEs and large enterprises in German IT.
- **Current ranges (2025-2026):** Junior ~48-55k, Mid ~57-70k, Senior ~70-85k, Lead/Principal 85k+. Wide variance by source.
- **Refresh cadence:** Annual comprehensive reviews with quarterly spot-checks for volatile markets. Monthly is aggressive but defensible for fast-moving IT.
- **Account for Tarifvertraege.** Collective agreements set legal floors in some sectors; check applicability.
